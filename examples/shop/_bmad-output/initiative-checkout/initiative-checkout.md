# Shoppers check out online

## Description

A shopper builds a cart, applies a discount and pays on the live site.

## Requirements

R1 (prd FR-1): Every price a shopper sees comes from one pricing function.
R2 (prd FR-2): A valid discount code lowers the total; an invalid one is refused with its reason.
R3 (prd FR-3): A shopper adds, changes and removes cart items and the total follows.
